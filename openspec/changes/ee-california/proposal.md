## Why

The repository currently exposes two incompatible interpretations of the 2020 abandonment boundary. The legacy Mode path in `Process.ipynb` and `function/cropland_abandonment.py` searches the full 1992--2022 sequence, so 2021--2022 can create or extend an event that is later projected back to 2020. The newer S0 path stops at 2020 but uses a different stable-cropland baseline and recultivation rule. Neither path provides the requested six-parameter `run_abandonment_detection(...)` contract or the complete source/mask-driven detector chunks plus CONUS prediction products needed to compare four- and six-year definitions at 300 m and 1 km.

The California reference and Earth Engine lanes also need a clearer authority split. P2 must remain an independent holdout, the RF must remain the existing annual crop/noncrop classifier rather than become a direct-abandonment model, and any post-freeze scene gallery must remain descriptive. Full California RF training is now conditionally authorized, but only when a measured canary and fresh commercial evidence prove a conservative all-in upper bound no greater than USD 100.00.

## What Changes

- **BREAKING**: establish `run_abandonment_detection(target_nc, window_year, start_year, current_end_year, init_cropland, extend_validation)` as the only public local detection entry point. Its canonical defaults are `D:\xarray\reclass_lccs_1km.nc`, `5`, `1992`, `2020`, `2`, and `True`.
- **BREAKING**: make `current_end_year` a hard event-discovery and measurement cutoff. With the default input, 2021 and 2022 may only validate post-cutoff persistence; they may not create an event, complete the five-year rule, move the exit year, extend the 2020 duration, or alter a 2020 label.
- Define `init_cropland=2` as a count of consecutive crop-class-1 years, not land-cover class 2. It governs both eligibility at `start_year` and the minimum consecutive crop prefix immediately before a candidate exit, preserving the two old legacy predicates under one explicit parameter.
- Define `extend_validation=True` as an exact two-year validation at `current_end_year + 1` and `+2`. Both years must be finite, valid, noncrop, and nonbuilt for a 2020-positive event to be retained in the final chunks. Missing extension years fail closed. `False` prevents post-cutoff years from participating at all.
- Parameterize the minimum sustained-noncrop rule through `window_year`; remove every hidden hard-coded five-year detector, candidate-mask, QA, and output-attribute assumption. Four and six years refer to this minimum duration, not to an even-sized temporal-majority filter.
- Run one declared local robustness family: `300m_min4`, `300m_min6`, `1km_mode_min4`, `1km_mode_min5`, and `1km_mode_min6`. The 300 m source is `D:\xarray\reclass_lccs_300m_esa_cci.nc`; the 1 km source is `D:\xarray\reclass_lccs_1km.nc` and uses categorical mode. Detector and merged NetCDF chunk inventories follow the fingerprinted source/mask footprint. For each feature's `us_abandon_clean_{feature}.csv`, candidate points are then limited to `lon=[-125,-65]`, `lat=[25,49]` and retained only by an inner `sjoin(..., predicate="within")` against `data/cb_2018_us_state_500k.shp` after excluding `STATEFP` values `02,15,60,66,69,72,78`. This includes DC and makes state-polygon membership, not the bbox, authoritative for the CSV.
- Require each local feature run to materialize and verify all three product families: `data/us_abandon_clean_{feature}.csv`, `D:\xarray\abandon_2_{feature}\chunk_{row}_{col}.nc`, and `D:\xarray\merged_chunk_2_{feature}\chunk_{row}_{col}.nc`. The spelling `chunk` is canonical. Existing unfeatured roots and prior attempts are immutable and may not be overwritten.
- Clarify that `us_abandon_clean_{feature}.csv` is the complete CONUS 2020 abandonment candidate/prediction table under that exact bbox/state rule, not the RF training table. It preserves the established prediction-table schema; the companion state-membership frame returned by `build_prediction_embedding` is deterministically rebuilt during acceptance, and its row-key/state mapping hash and counts are bound in the manifest. California P2/RF/evaluation consumes only the exact `STATEFP=06` subset reconstructed by the same join. Native 300 m rows remain native and bind their mapping to the 1 km `Feature_all` master grid with grid row/column, match distance, and pixel area.
- Require a machine-readable local-run acceptance manifest and an explicit function return receipt. A successful Python return or passing contract test is insufficient without present, schema-valid, hash-verified CSV and NetCDF artifacts for the declared feature, except for an explicitly validated zero-event result with a complete chunk inventory.
- Preserve California P1/P2 as a pre-model reference lane. The 36 core records are the only primary holdout denominator; the eight legacy 2017/2018 records remain a separate boundary appendix and the 12 blind-secondary reviews remain reviewer-QA evidence. P2 labels and review outcomes never enter RF training, threshold choice, window choice, resolution choice, candidate selection, or the CONUS local-product construction.
- Freeze a rule-neutral P2 evidence contract and a `decision_contract_family_sha256` before any model or variant result is revealed. The freeze deterministically projects `qualifies_min4_2020`, `qualifies_min5_2020`, and `qualifies_min6_2020`; the existing five-year category remains only a backward-compatible view.
- Preserve the Earth Engine model as a frozen annual binary crop/noncrop Random Forest producing `p_crop`. Four-, five-, and six-year abandonment labels are temporal projections of one frozen annual probability/model family; the RF is not retrained per duration and is not presented as a direct-abandonment classifier.
- Conditionally authorize full California RF training only after P2 acceptance, a checksum-verified minimal GEE-to-GCS-to-D-drive canary, an exact state-intersecting task manifest, and a fresh budget acceptance prove `safe_total_upper_usd <= 100.00`. The bound includes compute, all authorized attempts including failures, GCS operations and storage, and download/egress or any other paid component. Missing or stale price, EECU, balance, quota, capacity, task, or delivery evidence blocks execution.

- Replace the former P2-terminal task registry with one operative R5--R12 chain covering the local semantic correction, notebook parity, pre-model reference freeze, complete local products, conditional RF execution, post-freeze evaluation/gallery, and final product verification. Prior sealed R1--R4 wording remains provenance under the old fingerprint and is not executable under the new fingerprint.
