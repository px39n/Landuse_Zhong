## Accepted material decisions

### D1 — ACCEPT and execution chapter

- Decision: ACCEPT is the chapter-opening ruler; a chapter is the whole current
  active-registry fingerprint, not a task or attempt.
- Assumption: one fingerprint can safely govern many refs and attempts.
- Loophole closed: treating each ACCEPT line or Apply as an independent
  revision budget.
- Measurable outcome: promotion preserves the normalized ACCEPT/fingerprint.
- Falsifier: checkbox, Verify, or one task consumes a cycle stamp.

### D2 — Cycle versus chapter-outside stamp

- Decision: apply-revision, in-execution semantic reseal, and second/later
  same-chapter semantic reseal are charged; the first pre-execution or
  post-drain confirmed interviewer stamp is free.
- Assumption: fully drained means no ready, blocked, deviated, or in-progress
  ref remains.
- Loophole closed: `--confirmed` silently making an in-loop amendment free.
- Measurable outcome: the fourth charged stamp at default 3 is rejected before
  writes, while narrative and qualifying chapter-outside stamps continue.
- Falsifier: a confirmed in-loop stamp is free, or initial ACCEPT is charged.

### D3 — Admission and completion

- Decision: `max_revisions` is checked only before charged semantic stamps.
  Ordinary gate never rechecks it; closure ignores minutes and generic
  breakers.
- Assumption: current fingerprint in loop.json is sufficient admission proof.
- Loophole closed: Apply admitted before record and Verify denied after record.
- Measurable outcome: synthetic public/GDP-shaped fixtures remain drainable
  under high historical counts.
- Falsifier: same-fingerprint Verify or later ref requires a cap raise.

### D4 — Default settings

- Decision: new/omitted policy defaults are Apply 2, Unblock 2, cycle stamps 3;
  prior explicit values inherit.
- Assumption: task growth consumes per-ref Apply and active time, not cycle
  stamps calculated from task count.
- Loophole closed: emergency VFD values becoming repository defaults.
- Measurable outcome: new init writes 2/2/3 and fixture reseal preserves 1/5/14.
- Falsifier: max revisions is derived from N_tasks or prior policy is overwritten.

### D5 — Blocking-window self-restamp

- Decision: one same-ref, same-source-episode supervised self-restamp is allowed
  only in a blocking/amend_spec window, with a required reason and a
  mechanically non-widening candidate. It is charged when the cycle rules
  apply.
- Assumption: conservative rejection is preferable when natural-language
  narrowing cannot be proved.
- Loophole closed: Apply workers editing tasks, a second self-confirm, framework
  edits, or shrinking acceptance to turn failure into PASS.
- Measurable outcome: one allowlisted candidate succeeds without confirmed;
  widening/other-ref/policy/repeat cases keep all authority files unchanged.
- Falsifier: self-restamp changes any budget, DAG, scope, other ref, or passed
  ACCEPT.

### D6 — Governance and delivery

- Decision: every active call aligns ACCEPT, capability requirements,
  `loop.json`, canonical skill, and action class. The check adds no command or
  receipt. R1/R2/R3 are direct local `zpy`; no R4 or live VFD tree is part of
  G1.
- Assumption: three write-disjoint refs cover runtime, governance, and final
  sync without another lifecycle.
- Loophole closed: dual supervisors, automatic lanes, VFD product execution, or
  a second specification merge system.
- Measurable outcome: mirrors are byte-exact, sync is idempotent, strict
  validation/design verification pass, and a draft PR targets main.
- Falsifier: a new skill package, Cursor projection, VFD mutation, non-draft PR,
  or automatic merge appears.
